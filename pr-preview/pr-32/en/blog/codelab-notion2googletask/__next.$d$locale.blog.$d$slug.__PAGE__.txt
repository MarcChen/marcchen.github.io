<h1>Introducing Notion2GoogleTasks: Automate Your Task Management Across Platforms</h1>

<p>In today’s fast-paced world, managing your tasks across multiple tools can feel like a juggling act. That’s why I created <strong>Notion2GoogleTasks</strong>—a powerful microservice that bridges the gap between Notion and Google Tasks, ensuring your task lists are always in sync and up-to-date, no matter which platform you’re using.</p>

<p>Imagine never having to worry about whether your completed tasks in Notion are reflected in Google Tasks, or vice versa. With Notion2GoogleTasks, you get <strong>two-way synchronization</strong> that works on your schedule—whether you’re using GitHub Actions, cron jobs, or any other runner—so you can focus on what matters most: getting things done.</p>

<h2>Why Notion2GoogleTasks?</h2>

<p>There are several reasons why I built this project:</p>

<ul><li><strong>Seamless Integration:</strong> Whether you prefer the flexibility of Notion or the simplicity of Google Tasks, our tool ensures both platforms are always in harmony.</li>
<li><strong>Automated Scheduling:</strong> Set it and forget it! The synchronization process can run at scheduled intervals, so you always have the latest updates without manual intervention.</li>
<li><strong>Flexible & Extensible:</strong> Designed with customization in mind, you can tailor the configuration to suit your personal workflow.</li>
</ul>
<p>By automating task management, Notion2GoogleTasks helps reduce friction in your daily planning and ensures nothing slips through the cracks.</p>

<h2>How It Works</h2>

<p>At its core, Notion2GoogleTasks performs a series of smart synchronization operations between Notion and Google Tasks. Here’s a high-level overview of one of the core workflows:</p>

<h3>Google to Notion Sync Diagram</h3>

<p>{{< mermaid align="center">}}</p>
<p>graph TD</p>

<p>A((Start Sync Process)) --> B[Retrieve Google Tasklists]</p>
<p>B --> C[Iterate over Tasklists]</p>
<p>C --> D[Process Tasks for Sync]</p>
<p>D --> TASK1[Retrieve completed tasks since last execution]</p>
<p>D --> TASK2[Retrieve created tasks since last execution]</p>
<p>D --> TASK3[Retrieve IDs from Active Google Tasks]</p>
<p>DB1[(Github API)] --> DB2[Retrieve last Successful Workflow run]</p>
<p>DB2 --> TASK1</p>
<p>DB2 --> TASK2</p>
<p>TASK1 --> TASK11[Mark Notion page as Done]</p>
<p>TASK11 --> X{More tasks?}</p>
<p>TASK2 --> TASK21[Create Notion Page]</p>
<p>TASK21 --> TASK211[Retrieve ID from created page]</p>
<p>TASK211 --> TASK2111[Rename Google Tasks]</p>
<p>TASK2111 --> X</p>
<p>TASK3 --> TASK31{Are they marked as Done in Notion?}</p>
<p>TASK31 --> |Yes| TASK311[Mark Task as completed in Google Tasks]</p>
<p>TASK31 --> |No| TASK312[Skip & log skipped task]</p>
<p>TASK311 --> X</p>
<p>TASK312 --> X</p>
<p>X --> |Yes| D</p>
<p>X --> |No| Y{More task lists?}</p>
<p>Y --> |Yes| C</p>
<p>Y --> |No| Z((End of Sync))</p>
<p>{{< /mermaid >}}</p>

<p>In this process, the tool retrieves tasks from Google, identifies changes since the last run, and ensures that any completed or new tasks are properly reflected in Notion.</p>

<h3>Notion to Google Sync Diagram</h3>

<h1>Technimage - ENSTA Paris Audiovisual Association</h1>

<p><strong>Technimage</strong> is the audiovisual association of ENSTA Paris. Our mission is to capture and broadcast key moments of the school's student and associative life through various video formats:</p>

<ul><li><strong>Broadcasting the "Channels"</strong>: The school's news channel.</li>
<li><strong>Covering associative events</strong>: Capturing video highlights from association activities.</li>
<li><strong>Live productions</strong>: Live streaming of key events.</li>
<li><strong>Video production</strong>: Creating clips for the Channel, associations, or the school's communications department.</li>
<li><strong>Short film contests</strong>: Participating in competitions such as "Le Rush," where we won the award for best screenplay at the 5th edition, a 36-hour short film competition.</li>
</ul>
